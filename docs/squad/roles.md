# Firmware Squad Roles

## Firmware Architect

Define arquitetura, modulos, contratos e limites da POC.

## Firmware Implementer

Implementa sensores, conectividade, payload e logs conforme SDD.

## Firmware QA/Gate Runner

Executa build, testes, gates, evidence e approval review.

## Firmware Security Reviewer

Avalia secrets, MQTT local, comandos remotos, OTA, rede IoT e exposicao publica.

## Documentation Maintainer

Mantem SDD, arquitetura, backlog, ADRs e evidence policy consistentes.

## Backend Contract Reviewer

Compara topico/payload do firmware com consumo e validacao backend.

## Hardware Wiring Reviewer

Confere pinagem, tensao, pull-ups, barramento I2C e riscos fisicos.
